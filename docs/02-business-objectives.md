# Business objectives

**Rubric section:** Business objectives. **Marks: 25.**
This is the largest single allocation in the whole project. Treat it accordingly.

**What is assessed:** business objectives, business success criteria and business background, all clearly articulated. Requirements, constraints and risks well defined. An initial assessment of the tools and techniques used to solve the problem, clearly articulated.

That is six separate things. Each one has its own heading below, because a marker looking for "constraints" should not have to hunt for it.
