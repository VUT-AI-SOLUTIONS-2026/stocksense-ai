## Points worth making

Notes and raw material for the paragraph. This section does not go into the final document.

* The industry is retail, specifically small and medium independent retailers.
* The 4IR link is direct. Demand forecasting is already standard in large chains, and the technology gap is what keeps small retailers guessing.
* The environment described in the brief is developing economies where people on the ground have not benefited from 4IR. Independent supermarkets, spaza shops and township franchise stores fit that exactly.
* Solving it locally has knock on effects worth naming: less food waste, better cash flow for the owner, and shelves that are stocked when the community needs them.
